Say Goodbye to Household Insects

Ants marching through the kitchen, roaches behind the refrigerator, spiders in the basement, fleas on your pets — household insects are more than a nuisance. They contaminate food, trigger allergies, and multiply fast. Our trained exterminators identify the insect, locate the source of the infestation, and eliminate it with targeted, child and pet safe treatments — with minimum disruption to your daily life.

For over 30 years, Total Pest Control has been the insect exterminator Chicago homeowners and businesses trust. Using integrated pest management (IPM) methods, we treat the infestation you can see and the conditions that caused it — entry points, moisture, and food sources — so the insects don't come back.
