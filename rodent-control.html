Get Rodents Out and Keep Them Out

A mouse can squeeze through a gap the size of a dime, and a rat through a hole the size of a quarter. Once inside, rodents contaminate food, chew through insulation and electrical wiring, and reproduce at an alarming rate, since a single pair of mice can produce dozens of offspring in a year. Their droppings and urine can spread disease to your family and pets, which is why a rodent problem should never wait.

Total Pest Control has protected Chicago homes, restaurants, and businesses from rodents for over 30 years. We eliminate the active infestation with non-poisonous indoor methods and discreet, tamper-resistant exterior bait stations, then seal the entry points rodents use to get inside. It's a complete approach that's safe around children and pets, and it's guaranteed, with no contract required.
