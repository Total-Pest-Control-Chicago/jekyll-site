Take Back Your Yard, Deck & Pool

Stinging insects send hundreds of thousands of people to the emergency room every year. Yellow jackets nest in the ground and inside wall voids, hornets build paper nests in trees and under eaves, and wasps return to the same sheltered spots season after season. Knocking down a nest with a broom or a can of spray is how people get hurt — an agitated colony will defend itself aggressively, and some nests hold thousands of insects.

Our stinging-insect specialists have removed nests across Chicago for over 30 years — from eaves, decks, sheds, wall voids, attics, and playgrounds. We remove the nest safely, treat the area to eliminate returning foragers, and offer wasp-proofing that stops new colonies from forming in the same spots. Honey bees are handled responsibly, with live relocation whenever possible. Every job is guaranteed, with free estimates and no contract required.
