Evict Uninvited Wildlife Humanely

Scratching overhead at dawn, thumping in the walls, a strange smell from the chimney. Urban wildlife loves the shelter of a warm attic or crawl space. Left alone, raccoons tear up ductwork and insulation, squirrels gnaw electrical wiring into a fire hazard, and bat colonies leave behind droppings that can pose serious health risks. Wildlife problems only grow, especially in spring when mothers move in to raise their young.

Total Pest Control has handled wildlife removal for Chicago-area homeowners for over 30 years. We identify the animal, remove it humanely, taking care to keep mothers and their young together, and then wildlife-proof your home by sealing rooflines, vents, chimneys, and every other entry point. The animals leave, the damage stops, and they don't come back. Free estimates on every job, with no contract required.
